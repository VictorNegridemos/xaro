ADDENDUM TO THE YMIR-9 SALVAGE LOG
Second recorder card, recovered two weeks later.

The second card confirms that Dr. Halvard Quist signed his later entries as H. Quist. He survived the Kessler blackout and stayed aboard for another four days. His final entries say he no longer feared the Pale Chorus and had begun to transcribe its singing as musical notation. The notation itself is missing.

Quist's status in the Combine records is listed as "missing, presumed dead", although the recorder suggests he left the tender of his own will.

The Pale Chorus was heard again near the wreck by the boarding team's own sensors, which places it outside the Ymir-9 and not only in the crew's minds.

The boarding team's pilot, Ensign Corvin Hale, refused to dock a second time and filed a formal complaint with the Meridian Salvage Combine.
